# OLC project code and inspiration

Each team develops its own question. Use this guide to find examples, data sources and ideas that could support your investigation or a future project. You do not need to complete another repository to succeed in the hackathon.

## Start with your question

| Interest | Repository | What to look for or explore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This workshop: drought, streamflow and stewardship | [Climate Resiliency and Digital Sovereignty Learning Lab](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Education-Climate-Resiliency-Digital-Sovereignty) | The three supported pathways, interpretation activities and seven presentation questions. |
| Finding environmental data | [Geospatial Datasets](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Geospatial-datasets) | Source catalog and access notes. Which dataset has the place, period and variable your question needs? |
| Species observations | [GBIF Species Occurrences](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Agriculture-gbif_species_occurrences) | Biodiversity record exploration. How do observation locations and dates affect what you can infer? |
| Water | [Pine Ridge Hydrology](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Hydrology-hydrology) | Hydrology analysis notebooks. What can available water records tell you, and what evidence is missing? |
| Agriculture and land health | [Tribal Agricultural Science](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Agriculture-Tribal-Agricultural-Science) | Public-data analyses and project ideas connecting agriculture and land health. Read the current status and limitations before adapting an example. |
| Soils and geology | [Tribal Soils and Geology](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Geology-soils-geology) | Code and workflows for exploring soils and geological context. What soil or geologic information could help frame your question? |
| Geologic maps | [Historical Geologic Maps](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Geospatial-historical_geologic_maps) | A guided map notebook and example outputs. Compare map coverage, dates and source information. |
| Organizing observations with xarray | [Data Cube Tutorial](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Education-data_cube_tutorial) | Arrays, named dimensions and selecting observations through time. Further study for the optional cube extension. |
| Bounding Boxes | [Pine Ridge Bounding Boxes](https://github.com/olc-techsupport/Geospatial-Pine-Ridge-Bounding-Boxes) | Bounding boxes for Pine Ridge Reservation and individual communities. |

## Turning an example into your own project

1. State your team's question and why it interests you.
2. Identify one relevant notebook, method or data source from this guide.
3. Explain what you would adapt: the place, time period, comparison or variable.
4. Identify additional data, skills, partners or review needed to make that adaptation useful.

Use these notes for the future-project question in your final presentation. Cite the code and original data sources you use, follow their licenses and data terms, and retain source, place, period, units and limitations with your results. Public availability does not authorize use or disclosure of community-held knowledge or sensitive locations.
