# Data Investigator extension cards

Complete one card after the default workflow works. Record the original choice, changed choice, result difference, and interpretation difference.

## Card 1: Time-window sensitivity

- **Prerequisite:** Parts 2–4 complete
- **Estimated time:** 30-60 minutes
- **Task:** Change one start year, rerun only the dependent cells in order, and compare trend direction, magnitude, and data coverage.
- **Done when:** You can explain why selecting a period is an analytical choice.

## Card 2: Gauge comparison

- **Prerequisite:** Streamflow data loaded
- **Estimated time:** 45 minutes
- **Task:** Compare two gauges separately.
- **Done when:** Your chart identifies gauge, units, period, missingness, and geographic limitation.

## Card 3: Weight sensitivity

- **Prerequisite:** Watershed health score computed
- **Estimated time:** 30 minutes
- **Task:** Create three plausible weight sets representing different priorities and compare composite scores.
- **Done when:** You can show whether the category changes and explain why no weight set is value-neutral.

## Card 4: Proxy audit

- **Prerequisite:** Community-assets section complete
- **Estimated time:** 30 minutes
- **Task:** List what roads can and cannot indicate about climate risk. Propose a better variable and its governance requirements.
- **Done when:** Your recommendation separates convenient availability from meaningful measurement.

## Card 5: Provenance card

- **Prerequisite:** Any saved figure
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes
- **Task:** Complete the minimum provenance record in `guides/data_governance.md`.
- **Done when:** Another team can identify the exact source, processing choices, limitations, and review status.
